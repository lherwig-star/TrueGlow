## 2 · What TrueGlow is — and what it is not

TrueGlow is a **styling and grooming app**. It analyses photos you take
yourself and suggests changes to grooming, haircut and style.

TrueGlow is explicitly **not**:

- a medical device,
- medical, dermatological or dental advice,
- nutrition or fitness advice,
- an assessment of your looks or attractiveness.

The app awards no grades, points or rankings and makes no diagnoses. If
something in the photos looks like it should be checked professionally, it
points you to a practice — nothing more.

**Suggestions are suggestions.** Whether you follow them is your decision. We
accept no liability for decisions you make on that basis, except in cases of
intent or gross negligence.
